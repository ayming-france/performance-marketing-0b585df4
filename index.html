Revenue is a company-wide outcome, so not a metric any single team owns.

A revenue metric fails as a marketing KPI, on three counts

01
Lagging, not Leading
A lagging indicator reports what has already happened. It can't be a KPI. By the time it moves, there's nothing left to steer.
02
The last leg, not ours
Sales run the final stretch and close on terms we don't set. You can't optimize an outcome you don't control.
03
Attribution, not contribution
When every team chases the same single revenue figure, it sparks a credit war instead of teamwork. What we really want is collaboration, not silos, by measuring each team on the stage it actually owns.

We need to measure marketing where it can improve the system: own its stage, spot what drives it, make the whole pipeline better.

Marketing acts on a system, not on an isolated number.
